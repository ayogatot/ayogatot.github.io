SnapStash — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Package ID: com.snaptash.app

SnapStash ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the SnapStash mobile application.

1. Photos & Camera Data (100% On-Device)

  • Camera Permission: SnapStash uses your device's camera solely when you tap the shutter button to capture an object for a sticker card.
  • On-Device AI Background Removal: All subject segmentation, background removal, die-cut sticker processing, and touch-up editing happen 100% locally on your device using Google ML Kit.
  • Local Storage: Your original photos, sticker cutouts, card names, and binder albums are stored locally in your device's private app storage. We do not upload, store, or transmit your photos to any external server.
  • Sharing & Gallery Export: Images only leave private app storage when you explicitly tap "Share to Socials" or "Save to Gallery".

2. Third-Party Services (Ads & In-App Purchases)

SnapStash uses the following third-party SDKs provided by Google:

  • Google AdMob: Displays rewarded and interstitial ads on the Free Tier. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID, coarse IP-based location, and ad interaction/diagnostic metrics. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android Settings (Settings → Google → Ads). See Google Partner Sites Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: Processes the optional one-time "SnapStash Pro" upgrade. Payment details are handled exclusively by Google Play.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

All collectible cards, photos, and binders are stored locally on your device. You can delete individual cards or binders inside the App at any time, or delete all app data by uninstalling SnapStash.

4. Children's Privacy

SnapStash does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the developer support email listed on the SnapStash Google Play Store page.
